The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Ethics

Regulation & investor protection

In the newsSebi allows mutual funds to net cash market obligations from April

Regulators like SEBI and IRDAI set the rules for how financial products are sold and who may invest in what, with protecting investors as the goal.

Exam angleCFA Standard III(C), Suitability: understand a client's objectives and constraints before recommending an investment.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsLIC Q2 net profit rises 20% to ₹12,076 crore, net premium income up 16.2%

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Economics

Forex reserves & intervention

In the newsParaguay President Santiago Peña resigns amid ueno bank collapse

The RBI keeps a stockpile of dollars and other assets. It sells dollars to steady the rupee when it falls too fast, and buys them when it rises.

Exam angleSterilised intervention offsets the effect on domestic money supply with opposite bond trades; unsterilised intervention lets the money supply change.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Goodwill in business combinations

In the newsAdani Ports to acquire European storage major Evos for €2.56 billion

When one company buys another, it books the target's assets and liabilities at fair value. Whatever it paid above that becomes goodwill on its balance sheet.

Exam angleInd AS 103: goodwill is not amortised but tested for impairment every year (Ind AS 36). A bargain purchase gain goes to OCI and capital reserve under the Indian carve-out.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsBlinkit Ambulance to charge ₹2,500 for non-emergency transfers amid alleged misuse of free services

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsSebi eases KYC norms for overseas investors, enables digital onboarding

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

About this paper

Frosty’s Ledger prints itself every morning at about 6 AM India time. A small program reads the public RSS feeds of 20 publishers, keeps what’s free to read, puts the same story from different papers together, and links every headline to the original article. Nothing here gets around a paywall: locked stories show only the headline and summary the publisher shares for free.
