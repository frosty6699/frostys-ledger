CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsIs Anthropic's mega IPO at risk over its $1.5 trillion valuation?

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsHonasa Consumer eyes hair colour entry as Q2 net profit jumps 87% to ₹73 crore

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsRupee falls 9 paise to 94.50 against US dollar in early trade

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

CA Final · Advanced Auditing & Ethics

Frauds & the auditor's duty

In the newsCBI books Kishore Biyani, Future Retail in alleged ₹904-cr bank fraud

Auditors don't guarantee that no fraud exists, but they must plan the audit so that material misstatement from fraud has a reasonable chance of being found.

Exam angleSA 240: reasonable, not absolute, assurance. Under Section 143(12) of the Companies Act, the auditor must report suspected fraud above ₹1 crore to the Central Government.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Related-party transactions

In the newsNCLT declares Videocon promoter Venugopal Dhoot bankrupt over ₹12,668 crore dues to SBI

Deals between a company and its promoters, directors or group firms can move value out of minority shareholders' hands, so the law makes them disclose and seek approval.

Exam angleInd AS 24 requires the relationships and transactions to be disclosed. Section 188 of the Companies Act needs board approval, and shareholders' approval above the thresholds.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsIndia's foreign exchange reserves drop by $2.69 billion to $717 billion

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
