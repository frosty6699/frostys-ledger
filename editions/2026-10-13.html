The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Economics

Inflation & price indexes

In the newsFood, fuel push retail inflation to 23-month high of 5.54% in September

Consumer price inflation measures how fast the cost of a typical household basket is rising. Stubborn inflation keeps central banks from cutting rates.

Exam anglePrice indexes tend to overstate true cost-of-living changes because of substitution, quality and new-product biases.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the news'Why only cancer drugs?': SC questions Centre on trade margin cap, seeks wider price regulation

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Portfolio Management

Mutual funds, SIPs & ETFs

In the newsMutual fund SIP inflows hit record ₹32,490 crore in September; equity inflows rise 31%

Funds pool many investors' money into diversified portfolios. A SIP invests a fixed amount each month, automatically buying more units when prices are low.

Exam angleWith a SIP the average cost per unit is the harmonic mean of the prices paid, which is never above their arithmetic mean.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsRBL Bank’s Q2 net profit jumps 170% YoY

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee opens over 1% higher at 95.73 on RBI measures to curb dollar demand

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Dividends & buybacks under the Act

In the newsICICI Prudential AMC declares Rs 30 interim dividend; Check record date, Q2 results

Company law limits how much cash a company can hand back, to protect its creditors.

Exam angleDividends only out of profits (Section 123). Buybacks capped at 25% of paid-up capital and free reserves, with debt no more than twice capital and free reserves afterwards (Section 68).
