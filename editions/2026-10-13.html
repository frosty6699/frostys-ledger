CFA Level I

Economics

GDP & the business cycle

In the newsIMF raises India's FY27 growth forecast to 7% on strong domestic demand

GDP measures everything an economy produces. Upgrades and downgrades to growth forecasts feed straight into expectations for company earnings.

Exam angleGDP = C + I + G + (X − M). A PMI above 50 signals expansion and works as a leading indicator.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsRBL Bank’s Q2 net profit jumps 170% YoY

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Portfolio Management

Systematic risk

In the newsRBI to continue efforts to curb rupee volatility; external factors driving currency fall: FM

In a broad sell-off most stocks fall together: a market-wide shock is a risk you can't diversify away by owning more shares.

Exam angleOnly systematic risk (beta) earns a premium in CAPM: E(R) = Rf + β × [E(Rm) − Rf].

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Leases on the balance sheet

In the newsIndigo signs $328 million finance deal with Citi for 8 Airbus aircraft buys

Airlines, retailers and hotels lease much of what they use. Under Ind AS 116 almost every lease shows up as an asset and a matching liability.

Exam angleThe lessee books a right-of-use asset and a lease liability. Rent becomes depreciation plus interest, which lifts EBITDA.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee jumps 82 paise to 94.96 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Dividends & buybacks under the Act

In the newsICICI Prudential AMC net profit up 6% on rise in AUM

Company law limits how much cash a company can hand back, to protect its creditors.

Exam angleDividends only out of profits (Section 123). Buybacks capped at 25% of paid-up capital and free reserves, with debt no more than twice capital and free reserves afterwards (Section 68).
