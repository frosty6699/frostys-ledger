The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Economics

The repo rate & policy transmission

In the newsRBI MPC Meeting Oct 2026 Highlights: RBI MPC hikes repo rate by 25 bps to 5.5%, changes stance to 'calibrated tightening'

The repo rate is what the RBI charges banks for overnight loans against government bonds. Raising it makes borrowing costlier across the economy to cool inflation; cutting it does the opposite.

Exam angleTransmission runs policy rate → bank lending and deposit rates → spending and investment → growth and inflation, with a lag. Policy is contractionary when the policy rate sits above the neutral rate.

Alternative Investments

Venture capital & startup valuations

In the newsSpaceX in talks with investors to borrow $40 billion to buy Nvidia chips

Startups raise money in rounds, and each round sets a price for the whole company. A unicorn is a private company valued above $1 billion.

Exam anglePost-money valuation = pre-money valuation + new investment. VC returns come from a few big winners that make up for many failures.

Equity Investments

Payments & network effects

In the newsSmall fee unlikely to impact UPI transaction volumes: RBI Governor on MDR

UPI moves money instantly between bank accounts. The MDR is the fee a merchant pays on each digital payment, and who bears it shapes how widely it is used.

Exam angleNetwork effects: each new user makes a platform more valuable to every existing user, which can build a strong competitive moat.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee falls 22 paise to 96.57 against US dollar post RBI rate increase

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsKalyan Jewellers logs 26% rise in Q2 revenue

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Final · Indirect Tax Laws

Customs & anti-dumping duty

In the newsBharti Airtel hikes postpaid tariffs by ₹50, adds free international roaming

Goods coming into India pay basic customs duty plus IGST. Anti-dumping and safeguard duties are extra levies that protect local producers from unfairly cheap imports.

Exam angleIGST paid on imports is available as input tax credit; basic customs duty is not, so it becomes part of the cost of the goods.
