The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsAnthropic IPO raises over $81 billion in largest offering on record, topping SpaceX

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Alternative Investments

Gold as a safe haven

In the newsGold hits 14-month low: Why are silver and copper prices falling too? Experts explain what investors should do now

Gold pays no interest, so it tends to shine when real interest rates fall or fear rises, and to lose its lustre when real yields climb.

Exam angleCommodity futures return = spot price return + roll return + collateral return.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsPaytm share price crash: Payment stock slides 7% on UPI MDR rollout delay; Pine Labs, One Mobikwik Systems also tumble

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the news'Let's move forward': Nvidia interim CEO Kress announces pay raises at first all-hands meeting since Huang's exit

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Leases on the balance sheet

In the newsTata Sons board reviews Air India performance amid mounting losses

Airlines, retailers and hotels lease much of what they use. Under Ind AS 116 almost every lease shows up as an asset and a matching liability.

Exam angleThe lessee books a right-of-use asset and a lease liability. Rent becomes depreciation plus interest, which lifts EBITDA.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee slides 8 paise to 93.49 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
