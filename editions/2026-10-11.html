The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Ethics

Regulation & investor protection

In the newsSEBI to issue guidelines on CAS in a week: Chairman Pandey

Regulators like SEBI and IRDAI set the rules for how financial products are sold and who may invest in what, with protecting investors as the goal.

Exam angleCFA Standard III(C), Suitability: understand a client's objectives and constraints before recommending an investment.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsD-Mart Q2FY27 result: Net profit rises 8.5% to ₹743 cr, revenue grows 17.8%

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsIndia-U.S. trade pact to be finalised keeping changed circumstances in mind: Piyush Goyal

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRBI opens special dollar window for oil firms as rupee comes under pressure

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Taxation

TDS: tax deducted at source

In the newsDividend Stocks To Watch This Week: TCS, Vedanta, Canara Robeco; Check Amount, Record Date

The payer deducts tax when making certain payments (salary, rent, fees, property purchases from NRIs) and deposits it with the government; the payee claims it as credit against their tax bill.

Exam angleMiss the deduction and the payer faces interest and can lose the deduction for that expense, on top of being treated as an assessee in default.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsSmall, mid-cap firms to outpace large caps in Q2FY27 earnings growth: Report

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.
