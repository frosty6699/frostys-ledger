CFA Level I

Equity Investments

Valuation & the P/E ratio

In the newsEx-Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang misled top lawyer on affair with Alice Huang

A P/E tells you how many rupees investors pay for each rupee of earnings. A high P/E means the market expects fast growth ahead.

Exam angleJustified forward P/E = (D₁/E₁) ÷ (r − g): it rises with growth and payout, and falls as the required return rises.

Economics

The current account

In the newsIndia's Oct merchandise trade deficit rose to one-year high of $39.88 bn

The current account adds up trade in goods and services plus income flows. A deficit has to be financed by foreign money coming in: FDI, portfolio flows or loans.

Exam angleCurrent account = (private saving − investment) + (taxes − government spending). A deficit means the country invests more than it saves.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsTata Motors PV posts ₹5,451-crore Q2 consolidated loss on JLR restructuring charges

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsSembcorp Green Infra IPO: Temasek-backed renewable energy company gets SEBI nod for ₹3,750 crore public issue

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsAI risks are the biggest threat to the financial system, Fed survey finds

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 7 paise to 94.05 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
