The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Economics

GDP & the business cycle

In the newsIMF raises India's FY27 growth forecast to 7% on strong domestic demand

GDP measures everything an economy produces. Upgrades and downgrades to growth forecasts feed straight into expectations for company earnings.

Exam angleGDP = C + I + G + (X − M). A PMI above 50 signals expansion and works as a leading indicator.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsSEBI extends IPO issue-size relaxation until March 2027

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsHyundai Motor group to invest $15.5 billion in India over 7 years

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Leases on the balance sheet

In the newsAdani's Mumbai airport puts Navi Mumbai flight shift on hold after airline pushback

Airlines, retailers and hotels lease much of what they use. Under Ind AS 116 almost every lease shows up as an asset and a matching liability.

Exam angleThe lessee books a right-of-use asset and a lease liability. Rent becomes depreciation plus interest, which lifts EBITDA.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRBI to continue efforts to curb rupee volatility; external factors driving currency fall: FM

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Taxation

How income is taxed

In the newsGovt open to debate on LTCG tax, STT ahead of Budget: FM Sitharaman

Income is grouped under heads like salary, business and capital gains, added up, reduced by allowed deductions, and taxed at slab rates.

Exam angleGross total income → deductions → total income → tax at slab rates → rebate, surcharge and cess. Under the new regime, most deductions are given up for lower rates.
