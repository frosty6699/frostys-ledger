CFA Level I

Economics

The Fed & global capital flows

In the newsFed holds rates steady despite concerns over inflation

The US Federal Reserve sets the price of dollars. When US rates rise, money tends to leave emerging markets like India for dollar assets, pressuring the rupee and local shares.

Exam angleInterest-rate differentials drive capital flows and exchange rates: tighter US policy usually strengthens the dollar against emerging-market currencies.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsAdani Ports Q2 profit rises 18% to ₹3,682 crore, raises FY27 guidance

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsOil prices fall as hopes of US-Iran peace talks ease supply fears

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsFactory output growth slows to 6.7% in September

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsJio Platforms Extends Gains After Stellar D-Street Debut; Market Cap Tops Rs 13.5 Lakh Crore

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 4 paise to 95.03 against US dollar during early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
