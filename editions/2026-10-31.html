Economics

Inflation & price indexes

In the newsReliance Retail Q2 results: Net profit falls 24% to ₹2,611 crore; revenue rises 6%

Consumer price inflation measures how fast the cost of a typical household basket is rising. Stubborn inflation keeps central banks from cutting rates.

Exam anglePrice indexes tend to overstate true cost-of-living changes because of substitution, quality and new-product biases.

Alternative Investments

Gold as a safe haven

In the newsIndia’s forex reserves fall by $1.269 billion to $719.748 billion

Gold pays no interest, so it tends to shine when real interest rates fall or fear rises, and to lose its lustre when real yields climb.

Exam angleCommodity futures return = spot price return + roll return + collateral return.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsJio Platforms Q2 profit rises 13.7% to ₹8,387 crore, revenue up 11.4%

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Indirect Tax Laws

Customs & anti-dumping duty

In the newsGovt extends customs duty exemption on cotton imports till December 31

Goods coming into India pay basic customs duty plus IGST. Anti-dumping and safeguard duties are extra levies that protect local producers from unfairly cheap imports.

Exam angleIGST paid on imports is available as input tax credit; basic customs duty is not, so it becomes part of the cost of the goods.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Goodwill in business combinations

In the newsSun Pharma Q2 net profit falls 36% on one-time charges, revenue rises 12%

When one company buys another, it books the target's assets and liabilities at fair value. Whatever it paid above that becomes goodwill on its balance sheet.

Exam angleInd AS 103: goodwill is not amortised but tested for impairment every year (Ind AS 36). A bargain purchase gain goes to OCI and capital reserve under the Indian carve-out.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsGold and silver price today, Oct 30: Check retail rates of 24K, 22K gold and 999 silver ahead of Dhanteras, Diwali

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.
