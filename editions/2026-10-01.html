Economics

Inflation & price indexes

In the newsUS dollar flat against peers after softer-than-expected inflation data

Consumer price inflation measures how fast the cost of a typical household basket is rising. Stubborn inflation keeps central banks from cutting rates.

Exam anglePrice indexes tend to overstate true cost-of-living changes because of substitution, quality and new-product biases.

Equity Investments

Payments & network effects

In the newsTraders' associations call off ‘No UPI Day’ protest on 2 October after assurance from Finance Minister Sitharaman

UPI moves money instantly between bank accounts. The MDR is the fee a merchant pays on each digital payment, and who bears it shapes how widely it is used.

Exam angleNetwork effects: each new user makes a platform more valuable to every existing user, which can build a strong competitive moat.

Fixed Income

Bond prices & yields

In the newsUS yields rise slightly, rate hike bets ease after inflation data

A bond's price and its yield move in opposite directions. When market yields rise, existing bonds paying lower coupons are worth less, so their prices fall.

Exam angle%ΔPrice ≈ −ModDur × Δyield + ½ × Convexity × (Δyield)². Longer-duration bonds fall more when yields rise.

Share issue costs in an IPO

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsBank lending rates mixed in August; fresh deposit rates fall: RBI

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

Capital budgeting
