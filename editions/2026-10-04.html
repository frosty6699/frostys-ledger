The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Economics

Liquidity operations

In the newsSebi examining position limits for non-agri contracts to boost liquidity

Besides setting rates, the RBI manages how much cash sits in the banking system: reverse repos soak up surplus cash, while repos and bond purchases inject it.

Exam angleOpen market operations: buying bonds adds bank reserves (easing); selling bonds drains them (tightening).

Ethics

Regulation & investor protection

In the newsSebi to soon issue framework on Closing Auction Session; receives over 3,500 comments

Regulators like SEBI and IRDAI set the rules for how financial products are sold and who may invest in what, with protecting investors as the goal.

Exam angleCFA Standard III(C), Suitability: understand a client's objectives and constraints before recommending an investment.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsLula or Bolsonaro: Wall Street braces for two wildly different results in Brazil election

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Leases on the balance sheet

In the newsAdani Airports To Begin Redevelopment Of Mumbai Airport's Terminal 1B North Section

Airlines, retailers and hotels lease much of what they use. Under Ind AS 116 almost every lease shows up as an asset and a matching liability.

Exam angleThe lessee books a right-of-use asset and a lease liability. Rent becomes depreciation plus interest, which lifts EBITDA.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Dividends & buybacks under the Act

In the newsUpcoming dividend, stock split, bonus shares this week: NMDC, PH Capital, MoldTek, GTV and others - Record date, ratio

Company law limits how much cash a company can hand back, to protect its creditors.

Exam angleDividends only out of profits (Section 123). Buybacks capped at 25% of paid-up capital and free reserves, with debt no more than twice capital and free reserves afterwards (Section 68).

CA Inter · Taxation

TDS: tax deducted at source

In the newsTCS, Infosys, Wipro shares ahead of Q2 results: Explained | What Accenture's strong earnings mean for Indian IT stocks

The payer deducts tax when making certain payments (salary, rent, fees, property purchases from NRIs) and deposits it with the government; the payee claims it as credit against their tax bill.

Exam angleMiss the deduction and the payer faces interest and can lose the deduction for that expense, on top of being treated as an assessee in default.
