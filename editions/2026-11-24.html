Economics

Forex reserves & intervention

In the newsWhy Donald Trump believed he needed to get Jensen Huang back on top at AI behemoth Nvidia

The RBI keeps a stockpile of dollars and other assets. It sells dollars to steady the rupee when it falls too fast, and buys them when it rises.

Exam angleSterilised intervention offsets the effect on domestic money supply with opposite bond trades; unsterilised intervention lets the money supply change.

Derivatives

Futures, options & F&O

In the newsCrude oil prices near three-month lows as rising supply offsets Hormuz threat

Futures lock in a price today for a trade later; options give the right but not the obligation. SEBI studies found about 9 in 10 individual F&O traders lose money.

Exam anglePut–call parity: S + P = C + PV(X). A long call plus a short put at the same strike behaves like a long forward.

Fixed Income

Bond prices & yields

In the newsPeterson: Here’s what Bessent’s about-face on Jensen Huang reveals about Trump and China

A bond's price and its yield move in opposite directions. When market yields rise, existing bonds paying lower coupons are worth less, so their prices fall.

Exam angle%ΔPrice ≈ −ModDur × Δyield + ½ × Convexity × (Δyield)². Longer-duration bonds fall more when yields rise.

Boards & independent directors

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

Control, subsidiaries & JVs

In the newsIIFCL IPO: Govt to part sale stake, company to issue fresh shares

Buying a stake can make the target a subsidiary, a joint venture or just an investment, and each is accounted for very differently in the buyer's consolidated statements.

Exam angleInd AS 110 control = power over the investee + exposure to variable returns + the ability to use that power. Joint ventures use the equity method (Ind AS 28).

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.
