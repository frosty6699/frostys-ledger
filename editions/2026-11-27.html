Tariffs & trade

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

Gold pays no interest, so it tends to shine when real interest rates fall or fear rises, and to lose its lustre when real yields climb.

Valuation & the P/E ratio

In the newsWho is Vardhaman Jain? Chennai entrepreneur donates 121 kg gold worth up to ₹206 crore to Tirupati temple

A P/E tells you how many rupees investors pay for each rupee of earnings. A high P/E means the market expects fast growth ahead.

Exam angleJustified forward P/E = (D₁/E₁) ÷ (r − g): it rises with growth and payout, and falls as the required return rises.

Boards & independent directors

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

CA Final · Indirect Tax Laws

Customs & anti-dumping duty

In the newsTariff threat likely to hasten India-US trade deal, says Jefferies report

Goods coming into India pay basic customs duty plus IGST. Anti-dumping and safeguard duties are extra levies that protect local producers from unfairly cheap imports.

Exam angleIGST paid on imports is available as input tax credit; basic customs duty is not, so it becomes part of the cost of the goods.

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.
