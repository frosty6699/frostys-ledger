CFA Level I

Fixed Income

Bond prices & yields

In the newsBinance says 90% of assets stolen in record hack have been replenished

A bond's price and its yield move in opposite directions. When market yields rise, existing bonds paying lower coupons are worth less, so their prices fall.

Exam angle%ΔPrice ≈ −ModDur × Δyield + ½ × Convexity × (Δyield)². Longer-duration bonds fall more when yields rise.

Revenue recognition

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

Boards & independent directors

In the newsSebi pushes for resilient capital markets, focuses on investor protection: Chairman

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

Financial leverage

In the newsCarlyle Group, KKR get ready to snatch up bargain leveraged loans

Borrowing lets a company grow without issuing new shares, but interest has to be paid in good years and bad, which makes profits swing more.

Exam angleDegree of financial leverage = EBIT ÷ (EBIT − interest). Debt magnifies changes in EPS in both directions.
