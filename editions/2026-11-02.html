The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Economics

Foreign portfolio flows

In the newsBinance hack: Crypto exchange resumes withdrawals after more than $6 billion stolen, sees outflow of $4.7 billion

Foreign portfolio investors buy Indian shares and bonds through the market. Because they can sell quickly, their exits can hit the Nifty and the rupee at the same time.

Exam anglePortfolio investment is liquid, fast-moving money; FDI is a lasting ownership stake of 10% or more of voting power.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsAfter bumper IPO listing, Jio Platforms shares jump 3% post strong Q2 results | Should you buy, sell or hold?

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsOPEC+ set to keep December oil output targets steady amid war disruption

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsGross GST collections rise 23% to ₹2.31 trillion in October on strong sales

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.

CA Inter · Cost & Management Accounting

Contribution & break-even

In the newsApples new CEO Ternus to face first earnings test as memory costs surge

When input costs rise faster than prices, each unit sold contributes less towards fixed costs, so profit falls faster than sales.

Exam angleContribution = sales − variable costs. Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit; P/V ratio = contribution ÷ sales.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsReliance Industries Shares Jump Over 6% After O2C Drives Strong Q2 Results

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.
