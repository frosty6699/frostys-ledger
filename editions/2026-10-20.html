The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsJio Platforms' anchor book draws record ₹2.5 trillion in bids ahead of IPO

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Financial Statement Analysis

Bad loans & bank health

In the newsBajaj Finance Q2 net profit rises 29% to ₹6,367 crore on strong NII growth

A loan becomes a non-performing asset when repayments stop for 90 days. Banks then set aside provisions, which come straight out of reported profit.

Exam angleProvisions are an income-statement expense and shrink the net loan book on the balance sheet; watch the provision coverage ratio.

Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsIndia-New Zealand FTA comes into effect, removes tariffs on Indian exports

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Indirect Tax Laws

Customs & anti-dumping duty

In the newsUS is 'very close' to finalising a trade deal with India: Marco Rubio

Goods coming into India pay basic customs duty plus IGST. Anti-dumping and safeguard duties are extra levies that protect local producers from unfairly cheap imports.

Exam angleIGST paid on imports is available as input tax credit; basic customs duty is not, so it becomes part of the cost of the goods.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsDussehra 2026: Stock market holiday today or not? NSE, BSE to remain open or closed on 20 Oct Tuesday? Exchange calendar

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsZetwerk's $518 million India IPO fully subscribed on final day of bidding

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.
