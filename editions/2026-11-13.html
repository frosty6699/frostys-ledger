The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Economics

The repo rate & policy transmission

In the newsIndia’s retail inflation rises to 6.09% in October, breaches RBI’s upper tolerance limit

The repo rate is what the RBI charges banks for overnight loans against government bonds. Raising it makes borrowing costlier across the economy to cool inflation; cutting it does the opposite.

Exam angleTransmission runs policy rate → bank lending and deposit rates → spending and investment → growth and inflation, with a lag. Policy is contractionary when the policy rate sits above the neutral rate.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsKia India to invest $2 billion in second manufacturing plant in Andhra Pradesh

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsOil India's profit more than doubles on higher crude prices

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Advanced Auditing & Ethics

Frauds & the auditor's duty

In the newsTrump says US doesn't need Iran deal, asks Vance to tackle corruption

Auditors don't guarantee that no fraud exists, but they must plan the audit so that material misstatement from fraud has a reasonable chance of being found.

Exam angleSA 240: reasonable, not absolute, assurance. Under Section 143(12) of the Companies Act, the auditor must report suspected fraud above ₹1 crore to the Central Government.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Control, subsidiaries & JVs

In the newsAdyar Ananda Bhavan sells majority stake to Bodhi Tree for $180 million

Buying a stake can make the target a subsidiary, a joint venture or just an investment, and each is accounted for very differently in the buyer's consolidated statements.

Exam angleInd AS 110 control = power over the investee + exposure to variable returns + the ability to use that power. Joint ventures use the equity method (Ind AS 28).

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsZee Entertainment board approves ₹600 crore share buyback at ₹91 apiece

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.
