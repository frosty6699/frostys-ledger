The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Corporate Issuers

Mergers & synergies

In the news‘A good idea’: Elon Musk says special committees will discuss Tesla and SpaceX merger while he sits out

In an acquisition, the buyer is betting the combined business is worth more than what it pays, through cost savings or new growth: the synergies.

Exam angleSynergy = V(combined) − [V(acquirer) + V(target)]. Pay a premium bigger than the synergies and the value goes to the target's shareholders.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsJio Platforms IPO fully subscribed in less than 100 minutes of opening

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsUS State Secy Marco Rubio arrives in Jamnagar to visit Reliance refinery

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsCore sector growth at 30-month high of 7.9% in Sep as coal output rebounds

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Dividends & buybacks under the Act

In the news'MAKE DEALS': Trump says data centers should pay communities a 'dividend' to win support

Company law limits how much cash a company can hand back, to protect its creditors.

Exam angleDividends only out of profits (Section 123). Buybacks capped at 25% of paid-up capital and free reserves, with debt no more than twice capital and free reserves afterwards (Section 68).

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsRupee jumps to six-week high as Jio IPO-linked inflows bolster recovery

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.
