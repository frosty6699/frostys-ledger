CFA Level I

Economics

The Fed & global capital flows

In the newsTrump establishes committee to investigate Fed governor Lisa Cook

The US Federal Reserve sets the price of dollars. When US rates rise, money tends to leave emerging markets like India for dollar assets, pressuring the rupee and local shares.

Exam angleInterest-rate differentials drive capital flows and exchange rates: tighter US policy usually strengthens the dollar against emerging-market currencies.

Corporate Issuers

Buybacks & dividends

In the newsQ2 Result Updates: Poonawalla Fincorp Profit Surges To Rs 375 Crore; Anand Rathi Approves Dividend

Companies return spare cash through dividends or buybacks. A buyback shrinks the share count, spreading profits over fewer shares.

Exam angleA debt-financed buyback lifts EPS only if the earnings yield (E/P) is higher than the after-tax cost of debt.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsAmbani’s Jio said to set IPO price band at ₹1,065-₹1,119

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsAustralias Maas enters trading halt after Nvidia-backed Firmus shelves $5 billion IPO plan

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 23 paise to 96.65 against US dollar during early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Taxation

TDS: tax deducted at source

In the newsTCS share price jumps over 5% as investors cheer Q2 growth

The payer deducts tax when making certain payments (salary, rent, fees, property purchases from NRIs) and deposits it with the government; the payee claims it as credit against their tax bill.

Exam angleMiss the deduction and the payer faces interest and can lose the deduction for that expense, on top of being treated as an assessee in default.
