The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Economics

Inflation & price indexes

In the newsAmul, Mother Dairy hike milk prices by ₹2-4 per litre from November 12

Consumer price inflation measures how fast the cost of a typical household basket is rising. Stubborn inflation keeps central banks from cutting rates.

Exam anglePrice indexes tend to overstate true cost-of-living changes because of substitution, quality and new-product biases.

Fixed Income

Credit risk & ratings

In the newsMoody's raises India's FY27 growth forecast to 7% on broad-based momentum

Credit ratings grade a borrower's ability to repay. A downgrade raises its borrowing costs, and bondholders lose if it defaults.

Exam angleExpected loss = probability of default × loss given default. The credit spread pays for expected loss plus liquidity and uncertainty.

Portfolio Management

Mutual funds, SIPs & ETFs

In the newsEquity MF inflows fall 14% in October as mid-, smallcap investments cool

Funds pool many investors' money into diversified portfolios. A SIP invests a fixed amount each month, automatically buying more units when prices are low.

Exam angleWith a SIP the average cost per unit is the harmonic mean of the prices paid, which is never above their arithmetic mean.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsNoel Tata retires as Tata Investment chairman; Bahram Vakil to take over

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsKalyan Jewellers surges 10%, hits 52-week high post Q2 results

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Taxation

How income is taxed

In the newsNet direct tax collections rise 11.8% to ₹14.42 trillion as of November 10

Income is grouped under heads like salary, business and capital gains, added up, reduced by allowed deductions, and taxed at slab rates.

Exam angleGross total income → deductions → total income → tax at slab rates → rebate, surcharge and cess. Under the new regime, most deductions are given up for lower rates.
