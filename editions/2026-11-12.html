CFA Level I

Economics

Fiscal deficits & crowding out

In the newsNet direct tax collections rise 11.8% to ₹14.42 trillion as of November 10

The fiscal deficit is how much more the government spends than it earns. Financing it means issuing bonds, which competes with companies for the same pool of savings.

Exam angleCrowding out: heavy government borrowing pushes up interest rates and can squeeze private investment.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsOla Electric shares zoom 7% ahead of Q2 results; here's what to expect

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Fixed Income

Bond prices & yields

In the newsRupee falls 6 paise to 93.77 against US dollar in early trade

A bond's price and its yield move in opposite directions. When market yields rise, existing bonds paying lower coupons are worth less, so their prices fall.

Exam angle%ΔPrice ≈ −ModDur × Δyield + ½ × Convexity × (Δyield)². Longer-duration bonds fall more when yields rise.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsNvidia board meeting to decide CEO Jensen Huang’s fate stretches into evening

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsTop stocks to watch today: Tata Power, Bajaj Finance, HAL, IRCTC shares in focus on Thursday, 12 November | Full list

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsBajaj Finance launches ₹11,700 crore QIP at 5% discount to closing price

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.
