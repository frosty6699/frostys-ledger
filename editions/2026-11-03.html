CFA Level I

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsGovernment has fiscal headroom to absorb Rs 50,000 crore revenue loss from LTCG tax abolition: SBI Research

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsAnthropic revenue surges 1,127% to $17 billion in Q3, higher computing costs weigh on margins

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Alternative Investments

Venture capital & startup valuations

In the newsOracle raises $20 billion through stock and bond sales to fund AI push

Startups raise money in rounds, and each round sets a price for the whole company. A unicorn is a private company valued above $1 billion.

Exam anglePost-money valuation = pre-money valuation + new investment. VC returns come from a few big winners that make up for many failures.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Related-party transactions

In the newsWho is Darius Khambata? Harvard alumnus exits Tata Trusts, says September 17 events sullied Tata Sons' reputation

Deals between a company and its promoters, directors or group firms can move value out of minority shareholders' hands, so the law makes them disclose and seek approval.

Exam angleInd AS 24 requires the relationships and transactions to be disclosed. Section 188 of the Companies Act needs board approval, and shareholders' approval above the thresholds.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsITC Q2 net profit declines 18.7% as higher cigarette taxes hit earnings

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 12 paise to 94.72 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
