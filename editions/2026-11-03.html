Jobs & the labour market

Jobs data tell central banks whether an economy is running hot. Strong hiring can push up wages and inflation; layoffs signal slowing demand.

Exam angleUnemployment can be frictional, structural or cyclical; only cyclical unemployment moves with the business cycle.

Regulators like SEBI and IRDAI set the rules for how financial products are sold and who may invest in what, with protecting investors as the goal.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsApple posts all-time record quarter in India in Q4

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Control, subsidiaries & JVs

In the newsSBI Q2 results: Net profit jumps 14.3% to Rs 23,038 crore on stake sale gain

Buying a stake can make the target a subsidiary, a joint venture or just an investment, and each is accounted for very differently in the buyer's consolidated statements.

Exam angleInd AS 110 control = power over the investee + exposure to variable returns + the ability to use that power. Joint ventures use the equity method (Ind AS 28).

CA Inter · Cost & Management Accounting

Contribution & break-even

In the newsMarico share price: FMCG stock gains 8% on strong Q2 results, nears 52-week high - Buy or sell? Check share price target

When input costs rise faster than prices, each unit sold contributes less towards fixed costs, so profit falls faster than sales.

Exam angleContribution = sales − variable costs. Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit; P/V ratio = contribution ÷ sales.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 12 paise to 94.72 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
