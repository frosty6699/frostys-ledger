Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsHere’s the List of Goods the US, China Agreed on for Tariff Cuts

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsSensex today | Stock Market Highlights: Sensex tanks 1,124 points, Nifty ends below 22,800

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

Equity Investments

Payments & network effects

In the newsSC asks Centre to explain UPI charges above ₹2,000, refuses to stay levy

UPI moves money instantly between bank accounts. The MDR is the fee a merchant pays on each digital payment, and who bears it shapes how widely it is used.

Exam angleNetwork effects: each new user makes a platform more valuable to every existing user, which can build a strong competitive moat.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsIndian central bank likely shielding rupee as US-Iran impasses lifts oil prices, traders say

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsNSE shares slip below IPO price after lackluster India debut

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsNMDC commissions ₹5,427 cr integrated iron ore project in Chhattisgarh

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.
