The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsWhy Tata Sons faces a listing mandate and how Tata Trusts hopes to avoid it

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsIndia's Essar Group to build largest steel plant in US: Trump says 'largest plant in America by far'

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Economics

GDP & the business cycle

In the newsYouTube’s India ecosystem supports 9.6 lakh jobs, adds ₹18,000 crore to GDP

GDP measures everything an economy produces. Upgrades and downgrades to growth forecasts feed straight into expectations for company earnings.

Exam angleGDP = C + I + G + (X − M). A PMI above 50 signals expansion and works as a leading indicator.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsExclusive-Anthropic warns AI may pose existential risks to humanity in IPO filing

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee ends nearly flat as traders remain glued to oil moves, RBI caps fall

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsRBI Panel Recommends 11.2% Increase In States' WMA Limit To Rs 67,839 Crore

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.
