About this paper

The Daily Ledger prints itself every morning at about 6 AM India time. A small program reads the public RSS feeds of 20 publishers, keeps what’s free to read, puts the same story from different papers together, and links every headline to the original article. Nothing here gets around a paywall: locked stories show only the headline and summary the publisher shares for free.

Headlines, summaries and pictures belong to their publishers. The paper covers the last 30 hours; pictures are shown in black and white (hover to see colour), and links you’ve opened turn grey.
