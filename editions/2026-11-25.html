CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsAnthropic closes below $83 IPO price, but analysts are still largely positive

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Economics

Fiscal deficits & crowding out

In the newsUS prepares fresh tariff hikes on China and India over industrial subsidies; announcement expected this week

The fiscal deficit is how much more the government spends than it earns. Financing it means issuing bonds, which competes with companies for the same pool of savings.

Exam angleCrowding out: heavy government borrowing pushes up interest rates and can squeeze private investment.

Alternative Investments

Gold as a safe haven

In the newsGold, silver prices today 25 November, Wed: Latest 24K, 22K gold, 999 silver rates in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and others

Gold pays no interest, so it tends to shine when real interest rates fall or fear rises, and to lose its lustre when real yields climb.

Exam angleCommodity futures return = spot price return + roll return + collateral return.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee jumps 23 paise to 92.62 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsDrugmaker Insmed shares rally 14% after Sanofi agrees to buy it in pharma’s biggest deal in three years

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Final · Advanced Auditing & Ethics

Frauds & the auditor's duty

In the newsRBI proposes stricter rules against digital frauds

Auditors don't guarantee that no fraud exists, but they must plan the audit so that material misstatement from fraud has a reasonable chance of being found.

Exam angleSA 240: reasonable, not absolute, assurance. Under Section 143(12) of the Companies Act, the auditor must report suspected fraud above ₹1 crore to the Central Government.
