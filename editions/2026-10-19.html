The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsAnthropic commits 5% of its stock to new 'public benefit' fund ahead of IPO

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsHindustan Zinc Q2 Results: Net Profit Rises 17% To Rs 6,412 Crore, Margin Expands

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Alternative Investments

Venture capital & startup valuations

In the newsWorld Food Programme cuts aid to 500,000 Palestinians in Gaza, citing funding shortages

Startups raise money in rounds, and each round sets a price for the whole company. A unicorn is a private company valued above $1 billion.

Exam anglePost-money valuation = pre-money valuation + new investment. VC returns come from a few big winners that make up for many failures.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsPutin proposes summit with Trump, Xi Jinping to discuss Ukraine deal

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Cost & Management Accounting

Contribution & break-even

In the newsIndia sugar stocks slide as government halts sugarcane-based ethanol diversion

When input costs rise faster than prices, each unit sold contributes less towards fixed costs, so profit falls faster than sales.

Exam angleContribution = sales − variable costs. Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit; P/V ratio = contribution ÷ sales.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee falls 3 paise to close at 94.92 against US dollar

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
