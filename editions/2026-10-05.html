CFA Level I

Economics

Who really pays a tax

In the newsNext-Gen GST and India's next phase of growth

Taxes change behaviour: cuts leave more money to spend or invest, while indirect taxes like GST are ultimately paid through prices.

Exam angleTax incidence depends on elasticity: the less price-sensitive side of the market carries more of the burden.

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsOpec+ set to keep November oil output targets steady amid war disruptions

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsJio IPO likely by Oct end at enterprise valuation of above ₹12 trillion

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Inter · Taxation

TDS: tax deducted at source

In the newsRBI policy, TCS earnings, crude prices to steer markets this week

The payer deducts tax when making certain payments (salary, rent, fees, property purchases from NRIs) and deposits it with the government; the payee claims it as credit against their tax bill.

Exam angleMiss the deduction and the payer faces interest and can lose the deduction for that expense, on top of being treated as an assessee in default.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsGST exemption for banks, nominated agencies for gold, silver imports may be withdrawn

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
