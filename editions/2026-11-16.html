CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsCoal India's Mahanadi Coalfields seeks $9.55 billion valuation in IPO

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Economics

Foreign portfolio flows

In the newsTata Motors PV falls 7.5% after lowering JLR outlook

Foreign portfolio investors buy Indian shares and bonds through the market. Because they can sell quickly, their exits can hit the Nifty and the rupee at the same time.

Exam anglePortfolio investment is liquid, fast-moving money; FDI is a lasting ownership stake of 10% or more of voting power.

Alternative Investments

Gold as a safe haven

In the newsGold, silver prices today, 16 November: Check retail rates of 24K, 22K gold, 999 silver in Delhi, Mumbai and Kolkata

Gold pays no interest, so it tends to shine when real interest rates fall or fear rises, and to lose its lustre when real yields climb.

Exam angleCommodity futures return = spot price return + roll return + collateral return.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsChina's economy slows in Oct as factory output, retail sales miss forecasts

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 6 paise to 94.13 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Frosty’s Ledger prints itself every morning at about 6 AM India time.
