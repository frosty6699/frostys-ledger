CFA Level I

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsTCS Q2 results: Net profit rises 15% to ₹13,884 crore, revenue up 11.2%

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Economics

Who really pays a tax

In the newsGST Council meet: Scrapping tax officers' arrest powers, decriminalisation among reforms on agenda

Taxes change behaviour: cuts leave more money to spend or invest, while indirect taxes like GST are ultimately paid through prices.

Exam angleTax incidence depends on elasticity: the less price-sensitive side of the market carries more of the burden.

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsRupee rises 10 paise to 96.65 against US dollar in early trade

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsOla Electric sets ₹27 price for ₹1,000-crore rights issue

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsPC Jeweller vs Senco Gold vs Kalyan Jewellers - Best jewellery stock to buy after Q2 business update? Experts decode

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Taxation

TDS: tax deducted at source

In the newsTCS Q2 Results LIVE: TCS Q2 con. PAT up 15% y-o-y, GM Breweries sink post results

The payer deducts tax when making certain payments (salary, rent, fees, property purchases from NRIs) and deposits it with the government; the payee claims it as credit against their tax bill.

Exam angleMiss the deduction and the payer faces interest and can lose the deduction for that expense, on top of being treated as an assessee in default.
