CFA Level I

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsCanara Bank Q2 results: Profit rises 8% to ₹5,175 cr, revenue at ₹40,931 cr

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Economics

Inflation & price indexes

In the newsWholesale inflation jumps to 11.39% in September as food, fuel costs rise

Consumer price inflation measures how fast the cost of a typical household basket is rising. Stubborn inflation keeps central banks from cutting rates.

Exam anglePrice indexes tend to overstate true cost-of-living changes because of substitution, quality and new-product biases.

Fixed Income

Bond prices & yields

In the newsBank of America fined $1.2 billion over money laundering violations

A bond's price and its yield move in opposite directions. When market yields rise, existing bonds paying lower coupons are worth less, so their prices fall.

Exam angle%ΔPrice ≈ −ModDur × Δyield + ½ × Convexity × (Δyield)². Longer-duration bonds fall more when yields rise.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsHyundai Motor group to invest $15.5 billion in India over 7 years

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Control, subsidiaries & JVs

In the newsCCEA approves IDBI Bank stake sale to Fairfax for ₹52,878 crore; govt, LIC to receive proceeds

Buying a stake can make the target a subsidiary, a joint venture or just an investment, and each is accounted for very differently in the buyer's consolidated statements.

Exam angleInd AS 110 control = power over the investee + exposure to variable returns + the ability to use that power. Joint ventures use the equity method (Ind AS 28).

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 44 paise to 94.92 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
