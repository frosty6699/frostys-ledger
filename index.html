The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsBlackstone to invest ₹5,000 crore in IIFL Finance for 16% stake

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsAnthropic targets $1 trillion IPO valuation in test of Wall Street's AI fervour

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Economics

The repo rate & policy transmission

In the newsUS Fed’s Waller plans to support at least a quarter-point rate hike in December

The repo rate is what the RBI charges banks for overnight loans against government bonds. Raising it makes borrowing costlier across the economy to cool inflation; cutting it does the opposite.

Exam angleTransmission runs policy rate → bank lending and deposit rates → spending and investment → growth and inflation, with a lag. Policy is contractionary when the policy rate sits above the neutral rate.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Cost & Management Accounting

Contribution & break-even

In the newsMRF Q2 result: Profit falls 28% to ₹369 crore as raw material costs rise

When input costs rise faster than prices, each unit sold contributes less towards fixed costs, so profit falls faster than sales.

Exam angleContribution = sales − variable costs. Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit; P/V ratio = contribution ÷ sales.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsNvidia in Crisis! China Spy Allegations, FBI Probe, shares crash, more: Key things investors should know - Still a buy?

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsSoftBank shares climb 14% despite investment giant posting $7 billion quarterly loss on data-centre costs

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.
