The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsListing may change 'Tata way', put profits ahead of philanthropy: Noel

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Corporate Issuers

Buybacks & dividends

In the newsAshok Leyland Q2 Results: Profit Rises 12.7% To Rs 869 Crore, Dividend Declared; Check Record Date

Companies return spare cash through dividends or buybacks. A buyback shrinks the share count, spreading profits over fewer shares.

Exam angleA debt-financed buyback lifts EPS only if the earnings yield (E/P) is higher than the after-tax cost of debt.

Economics

Foreign portfolio flows

In the newsFPIs pump ₹84,290 crore into equities in first fortnight of November

Foreign portfolio investors buy Indian shares and bonds through the market. Because they can sell quickly, their exits can hit the Nifty and the rupee at the same time.

Exam anglePortfolio investment is liquid, fast-moving money; FDI is a lasting ownership stake of 10% or more of voting power.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsInvestors urge board to consider return of Jensen Huang, Musk says Nvidia will lose critical deals without its founder

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsPM Modi summons telecom secretary over fake court orders to block sites

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Financial leverage

In the newsBitcoin Tumbles to Three-Month Low as Liquidations Accelerate

Borrowing lets a company grow without issuing new shares, but interest has to be paid in good years and bad, which makes profits swing more.

Exam angleDegree of financial leverage = EBIT ÷ (EBIT − interest). Debt magnifies changes in EPS in both directions.
