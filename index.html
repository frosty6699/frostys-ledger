CFA Level I

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsTata Motors CV swings to ₹1,257 crore profit in Q2; revenue rises 36%

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsJio Platforms’ IPO becomes first public offer globally to cross 1 crore applications

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Alternative Investments

Gold as a safe haven

In the newsGold, silver prices today, 23 October: Check retail rates of 24K, 22K gold, 999 silver in Delhi, Mumbai and Kolkata

Gold pays no interest, so it tends to shine when real interest rates fall or fear rises, and to lose its lustre when real yields climb.

Exam angleCommodity futures return = spot price return + roll return + collateral return.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsZetwerk shares debut at 5% premium over IPO price, beat expectations

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsIndia's forex reserves decline $5 billion to $721 billion: RBI data

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Dividends & buybacks under the Act

In the newsInfosys Q2 results, dividend 2026 amount announced: ₹26 per share payout, profit jumps 6.23% YoY - Check record date

Company law limits how much cash a company can hand back, to protect its creditors.

Exam angleDividends only out of profits (Section 123). Buybacks capped at 25% of paid-up capital and free reserves, with debt no more than twice capital and free reserves afterwards (Section 68).
