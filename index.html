CFA Level I

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsCentre cuts windfall tax on diesel exports, raises levy on petrol

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

Fixed Income

Bond prices & yields

In the newsSupreme Court upholds Bombay HC order quashing Yes Bank’s ₹8,415 cr AT-1 bond write-off

A bond's price and its yield move in opposite directions. When market yields rise, existing bonds paying lower coupons are worth less, so their prices fall.

Exam angle%ΔPrice ≈ −ModDur × Δyield + ½ × Convexity × (Δyield)². Longer-duration bonds fall more when yields rise.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsTech Mahindra Q2 profit rises 32.7% to ₹1,586 crore; revenue up 15.6%

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 20 paise to 94.79 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsMukesh Ambani taps massive Reliance Industries workforce to boost record Jio IPO

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsFed Chair Warsh stresses price stability, offers no signals on rate outlook

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.
