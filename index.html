The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsReliance Brands FY26 revenue up 3.8% to Rs 3,390.9 crore; loss narrows to Rs 137 crore

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsMahindra Last Mile Mobility eyes IPO in 18 months; plans expansion into new products: Chairman

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsTrump threatens ‘tremendous tax’ on US firms building data centres abroad: Could India lose out?

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsGovt tightens terms for extending contracts of 7 pre-NELP oil, gas blocks

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsGST Network adds metro rail as fifth mode of transport in e-way bill system

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsChalet Hotels plans ₹3,500 crore capex over three years: CEO Shwetank Singh

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.
