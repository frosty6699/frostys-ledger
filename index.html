The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Economics

Foreign portfolio flows

In the newsMutual fund SIP inflows hit record ₹32,490 crore in September; equity inflows rise 31%

Foreign portfolio investors buy Indian shares and bonds through the market. Because they can sell quickly, their exits can hit the Nifty and the rupee at the same time.

Exam anglePortfolio investment is liquid, fast-moving money; FDI is a lasting ownership stake of 10% or more of voting power.

Derivatives

Futures, options & F&O

In the newsBanks seek RBI relief, clarity on India's latest rupee-defence measures

Futures lock in a price today for a trade later; options give the right but not the obligation. SEBI studies found about 9 in 10 individual F&O traders lose money.

Exam anglePut–call parity: S + P = C + PV(X). A long call plus a short put at the same strike behaves like a long forward.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsBonfiglioli Transmissions announces Rs 2,534-cr IPO; price band set at Rs 558-586/sh

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Goodwill in business combinations

In the newsAmazon in talks to acquire AI startup Decart for around $7 billion

When one company buys another, it books the target's assets and liabilities at fair value. Whatever it paid above that becomes goodwill on its balance sheet.

Exam angleInd AS 103: goodwill is not amortised but tested for impairment every year (Ind AS 36). A bargain purchase gain goes to OCI and capital reserve under the Indian carve-out.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee opens over 1% higher at 95.73 on RBI measures to curb dollar demand

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsEmcure Pharma up over 3% as board approves merger of two subsidiaries

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.
