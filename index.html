The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsFlipkart CEO Kalyan Krishnamurthy steps down after 11 years amid IPO limbo

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsUS proposes up to 25% tariff on India, offers 17% upon trade deal

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsMusk's SpaceX gets FCC nod for 1 million satellites to power orbital data centres

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Related-party transactions

In the newsLG Electronics shares to be in focus as promoter to sell 2% stake to meet public float norms; check details

Deals between a company and its promoters, directors or group firms can move value out of minority shareholders' hands, so the law makes them disclose and seek approval.

Exam angleInd AS 24 requires the relationships and transactions to be disclosed. Section 188 of the Companies Act needs board approval, and shareholders' approval above the thresholds.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee hits eight-month high as foreign inflows lift markets; Nifty nears record peak

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Indirect Tax Laws

Customs & anti-dumping duty

In the newsNasdaq 100 plunges nearly 5%, briefly enters bear-market zone as AI fears and fresh tariffs hit sentiment

Goods coming into India pay basic customs duty plus IGST. Anti-dumping and safeguard duties are extra levies that protect local producers from unfairly cheap imports.

Exam angleIGST paid on imports is available as input tax credit; basic customs duty is not, so it becomes part of the cost of the goods.
