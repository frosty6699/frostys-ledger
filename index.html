CFA Level I

Economics

The rupee & exchange rates

In the newsIndiGo Q2 net loss narrows 27.2% to ₹1,879 crore despite fuel cost surge

A weaker rupee means more rupees per dollar. Imports like oil and electronics get costlier, but exporters such as IT services earn more in rupee terms.

Exam angleMarshall–Lerner: a depreciation improves the trade balance only if export and import demand elasticities together exceed 1, and often only after a J-curve delay.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsNoel Tata to retire as Trent chairman on Nov 11; Bahram Vakil to take over

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Portfolio Management

Systematic risk

In the newsAmazon burns cash, Intel flags weak PC demand as AI boom’s costs ripple across markets

In a broad sell-off most stocks fall together: a market-wide shock is a risk you can't diversify away by owning more shares.

Exam angleOnly systematic risk (beta) earns a premium in CAPM: E(R) = Rf + β × [E(Rm) − Rf].

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 7 paise to 95.19 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsAsian Paints shares steady Q2 show, brokerages trim target prices

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsWall Street slides as Alphabet revenue miss revives AI capex jitters

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.
