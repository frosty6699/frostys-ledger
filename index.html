The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsIndia-New Zealand FTA comes into effect, removes tariffs on Indian exports

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

Alternative Investments

Oil prices & India's economy

In the newsIndia a ‘refining superpower’: Marco Rubio lands in Mumbai, says US to discuss processing Venezuelan crude with Reliance

India imports most of the oil it uses, so pricier crude widens the trade deficit, weakens the rupee and pushes up inflation, all at once.

Exam angleBackwardation (spot above futures) signals tight near-term supply; contango (futures above spot) signals plentiful supply and storage costs.

Fixed Income

Credit risk & ratings

In the newsApple’s 'Pro and Tell’ Event On October 28: MacBook Pro, iMac And iPad Mini Upgrades Expected

Credit ratings grade a borrower's ability to repay. A downgrade raises its borrowing costs, and bondholders lose if it defaults.

Exam angleExpected loss = probability of default × loss given default. The credit spread pays for expected loss plus liquidity and uncertainty.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Goodwill in business combinations

In the newsUS bans Chinese AI firm DeepSeek from government agency IT systems

When one company buys another, it books the target's assets and liabilities at fair value. Whatever it paid above that becomes goodwill on its balance sheet.

Exam angleInd AS 103: goodwill is not amortised but tested for impairment every year (Ind AS 36). A bargain purchase gain goes to OCI and capital reserve under the Indian carve-out.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsTitan Q2 results 2026 date: Check Rekha Jhujhunwala portfolio stock returns, target, support - Should you buy?

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRBI steps up scrutiny of large overseas investments as rupee slides

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
