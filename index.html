The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsAmazon plans $7 billion renewable energy investment in India by 2030

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsWall Street falters ahead of Nvidia earnings, Anthropic debut

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Equity Investments

Market efficiency

In the newsUS AI chip curbs threaten Southeast Asia data centre boom, shares slump

Share prices react within minutes of news because traders race to price in new information.

Exam angleSemi-strong-form efficiency: prices already reflect all public information, so trading on headlines alone shouldn't earn excess returns.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsNvidia scandal: Jensen Huang signals willingness to return as CEO

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsReliance plans to invest about $1.6 billion to build new biogas plants

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsDarius Khambata hits back at Venu Srinivasan, Vijay Singh, questions Tata Sons listing U-turn

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.
