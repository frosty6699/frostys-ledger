CFA Level I

Financial Statement Analysis

Bad loans & bank health

In the newsICICI Bank Q2 net rises 20% to ₹14,843 crore as provisions fall

A loan becomes a non-performing asset when repayments stop for 90 days. Banks then set aside provisions, which come straight out of reported profit.

Exam angleProvisions are an income-statement expense and shrink the net loan book on the balance sheet; watch the provision coverage ratio.

Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsIndia gets tariff reprieve as Trump waives Russian oil penalties, but the threat lingers

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

Regulation & investor protection

In the newsFSSAI proposes curbs on added sugar in infant food, pesticide limits in tea

Regulators like SEBI and IRDAI set the rules for how financial products are sold and who may invest in what, with protecting investors as the goal.

Exam angleCFA Standard III(C), Suitability: understand a client's objectives and constraints before recommending an investment.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsKaizad Bharucha to exit HDFC Bank at end of December after three decades

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsHDFC Bank Q2 net profit rises 4.7% to ₹19,510 crore as NII grows

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 20 paise to 94.79 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
