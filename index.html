The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsNothing imminent: USTR Greer on India trade deal; Modi, Trump to talk soon

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

Derivatives

Futures, options & F&O

In the newsGold steadies before US payrolls data, set for second weekly loss

Futures lock in a price today for a trade later; options give the right but not the obligation. SEBI studies found about 9 in 10 individual F&O traders lose money.

Exam anglePut–call parity: S + P = C + PV(X). A long call plus a short put at the same strike behaves like a long forward.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsIndia does not have structural excess manufacturing capacity: Piyush Goyal

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsIndia's manufacturing PMI rises to 55.1 in September, hits 7-month high

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsGross GST collections rise 14.7% to over ₹2.03 trillion in September

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsAdani projects worth ₹2.6 trillion completed or underway in Maharashtra

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.
