CFA Level I

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsCoca-Cola's India bottling arm HCCH files DRHP for 997.5 million-share IPO

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Corporate Issuers

Mergers & synergies

In the newsIOC to buy crude, LPG ships as West Asia crisis exposes shipping risks

In an acquisition, the buyer is betting the combined business is worth more than what it pays, through cost savings or new growth: the synergies.

Exam angleSynergy = V(combined) − [V(acquirer) + V(target)]. Pay a premium bigger than the synergies and the value goes to the target's shareholders.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsDivi's Labs Q2 result: Net profit rises 64% to ₹1,127 cr on higher revenue

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Control, subsidiaries & JVs

In the newsSBI expands US footprint as California subsidiary opens first Texas branch

Buying a stake can make the target a subsidiary, a joint venture or just an investment, and each is accounted for very differently in the buyer's consolidated statements.

Exam angleInd AS 110 control = power over the investee + exposure to variable returns + the ability to use that power. Joint ventures use the equity method (Ind AS 28).

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsNifty, Sensex log worst Samvat returns in 15 years. What lies ahead in 2083?

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

GST & input tax credit

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.
