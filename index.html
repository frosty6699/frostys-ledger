CFA Level I

Economics

Tariffs & trade

In the newsIndia, US have reached a plateau in trade talks, says FM Nirmala Sitharaman

A tariff is a tax on imports. It shields domestic producers but raises prices for consumers and often invites retaliation.

Exam angleFor a small importing country a tariff creates a deadweight loss: consumers lose more than producers and the government gain.

Portfolio Management

Systematic risk

In the newsFlydubai co-pilot planned crash before airline job, say Israeli officials

In a broad sell-off most stocks fall together: a market-wide shock is a risk you can't diversify away by owning more shares.

Exam angleOnly systematic risk (beta) earns a premium in CAPM: E(R) = Rf + β × [E(Rm) − Rf].

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsJio Platforms likely to launch IPO on October 21, seeks to raise $3.8 bn

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsNext-Gen GST and India's next phase of growth

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Share issue costs in an IPO

In the newsNew IPO listings: Discount or premium? AceVector, Runwal, Orient, German Green Steel & Power - Check worst, best debut

An IPO's costs — bankers, lawyers, listing fees — don't all hit the profit and loss account.

Exam angleInd AS 32: costs of issuing new shares are deducted from equity; costs of listing existing shares are expensed. Shared costs are split between the two.

Boards & independent directors
