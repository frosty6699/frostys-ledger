Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsApollo Hospitals Q2 net profit rises 32% to ₹630 crore; revenue up 23%

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Corporate Issuers

Mergers & synergies

In the newsUS midterms 2026: How Democrats’ Congress victory puts Trump’s AI data centre push, tech billionaires on notice

In an acquisition, the buyer is betting the combined business is worth more than what it pays, through cost savings or new growth: the synergies.

Exam angleSynergy = V(combined) − [V(acquirer) + V(target)]. Pay a premium bigger than the synergies and the value goes to the target's shareholders.

Economics

Inflation & price indexes

In the newsIndia's services PMI hits four-month high in October as demand picks up

Consumer price inflation measures how fast the cost of a typical household basket is rising. Stubborn inflation keeps central banks from cutting rates.

Exam anglePrice indexes tend to overstate true cost-of-living changes because of substitution, quality and new-product biases.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Leases on the balance sheet

In the newsIndiGo in talks with Embraer for 100 jets

Airlines, retailers and hotels lease much of what they use. Under Ind AS 116 almost every lease shows up as an asset and a matching liability.

Exam angleThe lessee books a right-of-use asset and a lease liability. Rent becomes depreciation plus interest, which lifts EBITDA.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee rises 14 paise to 94.61 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Taxation

GST & input tax credit

In the newsSupreme Court upholds states' power to levy additional sales tax

GST is collected at every stage of the supply chain, but each business claims credit for the GST it paid on its inputs, so tax falls only on the value it adds.

Exam angleCredit needs a valid tax invoice, receipt of the goods or services, the tax actually paid to the government by the supplier, and the buyer's return filed.
