The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsTrump threatens ‘tremendous tax’ on US companies building data centres abroad

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Economics

The rupee & exchange rates

In the newsRBI clarifies forex reporting rules for services imports and exports

A weaker rupee means more rupees per dollar. Imports like oil and electronics get costlier, but exporters such as IT services earn more in rupee terms.

Exam angleMarshall–Lerner: a depreciation improves the trade balance only if export and import demand elasticities together exceed 1, and often only after a J-curve delay.

Equity Investments

Market efficiency

In the newsAsian stocks head for worst week since 2020 as AI rally loses steam

Share prices react within minutes of news because traders race to price in new information.

Exam angleSemi-strong-form efficiency: prices already reflect all public information, so trading on headlines alone shouldn't earn excess returns.

CA · ICAI

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsIndusInd Bank Terminates Former CEO Sumant Kathpalia, Invokes Clawback From 2020

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsCore infrastructure output growth hits 31-month high of 10.9% in October

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsRupee strengthens 17 paise to 93.15 against US dollar in early trade

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.
