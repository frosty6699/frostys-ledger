The CFA & CA Lens

Today’s news, exam-style

CFA Level I

Corporate Issuers

Mergers & synergies

In the newsWhy is Elon Musk plotting a mega-merger of SpaceX and Tesla?

In an acquisition, the buyer is betting the combined business is worth more than what it pays, through cost savings or new growth: the synergies.

Exam angleSynergy = V(combined) − [V(acquirer) + V(target)]. Pay a premium bigger than the synergies and the value goes to the target's shareholders.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsJio Platforms IPO fully subscribed in 100 mins; booked 3.16x on Day 1

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsMultibagger penny stock: PC Jeweller zooms 9% post Q2 nos, debt-free status

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsUS orders some diplomats to leave Saudi Arabia as Houthi attacks escalate

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsAmbani family hosts Marco Rubio and Sergio Gor at Jamnagar refinery; Reliance, US explore energy partnership

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.

CA Inter · Financial Management

Capital budgeting

In the newsCore sector growth at 30-month high of 7.9% in Sep as coal output rebounds

A new plant or data centre is only worth building if the cash it throws off, discounted at the cost of capital, beats what it costs.

Exam angleAccept if NPV > 0. Payback period is simple but ignores the time value of money and cash flows after payback.
