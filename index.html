The CFA & CA Lens

CFA Level I

Fixed Income

The yield curve

In the news10-year Treasury yield hits highest level since 2002 as global bond rout gathers pace

The yield curve plots yields across maturities. When long-dated yields rise faster than short ones the curve steepens, often a sign of higher expected inflation or heavy government borrowing.

Exam angleKnow the three theories: pure expectations, liquidity preference and market segmentation. An inverted curve (short above long) has often come before recessions.

Economics

Fiscal deficits & crowding out

In the newsGross GST collections rise 14.7% to over ₹2.03 trillion in September

The fiscal deficit is how much more the government spends than it earns. Financing it means issuing bonds, which competes with companies for the same pool of savings.

Exam angleCrowding out: heavy government borrowing pushes up interest rates and can squeeze private investment.

Equity Investments

IPOs & the grey market

In the newsAnarock Property Consultants files draft papers for ₹1,000 crore IPO

An IPO is a company's first sale of shares to the public. The grey-market premium is an unofficial pre-listing price signal, not a promise of listing gains.

Exam anglePrimary market: the company raises new capital. Secondary market: investors trade existing shares and the company receives nothing.

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Revenue recognition

In the newsIndia's manufacturing PMI rises to 55.1 in September, hits 7-month high

Revenue is recognised when control of goods or services passes to the customer, not simply when cash arrives or a contract is signed.

Exam angleInd AS 115's five steps: identify the contract → performance obligations → transaction price → allocate it → recognise as each obligation is satisfied.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsSensex today | Stock Market Highlights: Sensex falls 571 points, Nifty slips 0.88% as auto stocks drag markets

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Cost & Management Accounting

Contribution & break-even

In the newsStocks to buy: Titan, Lenskart, Dabur among Nomura’s 17 consumer picks. Do you own any?

When input costs rise faster than prices, each unit sold contributes less towards fixed costs, so profit falls faster than sales.

Exam angleContribution = sales − variable costs. Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ contribution per unit; P/V ratio = contribution ÷ sales.
