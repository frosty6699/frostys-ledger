CFA Level I

Financial Statement Analysis

Earnings & margins

In the newsMusk says SpaceX raised $40 billion in debt deal, predicts neocloud shakeout

Results show whether a company is growing profits, not just sales. Margins show how much of each rupee of revenue survives as profit.

Exam angleGross margin → operating margin (EBIT) → net margin. EBITDA adds back depreciation and amortisation, which are non-cash expenses.

Corporate Issuers

Capex & project returns

In the newsPM Modi to inaugurate RBI's data centre in Bhubaneswar

Capital expenditure is money spent on long-lived assets like plants or data centres. It creates value only if its returns beat the cost of the money used.

Exam angleAccept projects with NPV > 0, which for conventional cash flows means IRR > the cost of capital (WACC).

Equity Investments

Market efficiency

In the news‘Here to keep a jihadist out’: Trump attacks Muslim Senate candidate El-Sayed at Michigan rally

Share prices react within minutes of news because traders race to price in new information.

Exam angleSemi-strong-form efficiency: prices already reflect all public information, so trading on headlines alone shouldn't earn excess returns.

CA · ICAI

CA Final · Financial Reporting

Leases on the balance sheet

In the newsIndiGo, Air India to shift flights to Navi Mumbai ahead of T1 revamp

Airlines, retailers and hotels lease much of what they use. Under Ind AS 116 almost every lease shows up as an asset and a matching liability.

Exam angleThe lessee books a right-of-use asset and a lease liability. Rent becomes depreciation plus interest, which lifts EBITDA.

CA Final · Advanced Financial Management

Hedging currency risk

In the newsCrude oil prices, US Fed policy decision to steer rupee, bonds this week

Exporters and importers use forward contracts to lock in today the exchange rate they'll get when the payment actually arrives.

Exam angleForward rate by interest rate parity (direct quote): F = S × (1 + r_home) ÷ (1 + r_foreign). An exporter hedges by selling dollars forward.

CA Inter · Corporate & Other Laws

Boards & independent directors

In the newsGovernment needs to incentivise domestic solar production as China eyes curbs on tech exports: Solex Chairman

The board is accountable to shareholders for running the company. Independent directors are meant to protect minority shareholders when management and promoters disagree with them.

Exam angleSection 149 of the Companies Act: listed public companies need at least one-third independent directors on their board.
